Do not check durations against the brief's timecodes in JTI03, and do not mention a difference in reports, open-item lists or messages to the agency. A difference of 10-15 s (whole video or scene) does not matter.

**Why:** the user said so twice (2026-11-03 and 2026-11-10): the brief timing is approximate. On 2026-11-10 they had to remove such an item ("PV03 is 2:13 instead of 2:01") from a message to Alina.

**How to apply:** fit scenes to the voice-over and interviews, not to the brief clock; leave timing out of check-lists, recaps and drafts for the client/agency. Only raise it if a video becomes far off (minutes, not seconds) or the user asks. See [[jti03-voiceover-versions]].
